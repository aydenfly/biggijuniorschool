About Biggi Junior School

An ultra-modern school for a brighter future

Biggi Junior School is based in Pakwach. Established in 2017, we have grown to over 200 registered pupils, offering a full education from Nursery through Primary 7 in an environment built on discipline, care, and community.

Our teachers and leadership work as one team to keep classrooms full of curiosity, and to make sure that a family's circumstances never decide whether a child gets to learn. Through fundraising and bursary support, we continue extending that promise to more children each year.

Tuition & Fees

Fees, bursaries, and how to give

School fees cover tuition, meals, and learning materials for each term. Detailed termly fee structures by class are shared directly with parents at enrolment and can be requested through the Contacts section below.

Every term, some of our brightest pupils risk missing class simply because their families cannot afford tuition or scholastic materials. That's why we run a bursary programme, supported by donors and well-wishers, so that no child's circumstances decide whether they get to learn.

  • Fund tuition for a pupil in need
  • Provide scholastic materials — books, pens, uniforms
  • Sponsor a full bursary for a term or a year
  • Support general school running costs
