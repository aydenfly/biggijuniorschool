Education for a Brighter Future

Biggi Junior School is an ultra-modern junior school based in Bweya, Kajjansi Town Council, Wakiso district. It was established in 2017 and currently has over 200 registered students.

We have classes beginning all the way from Nursery up to Primary 7. Our curricular and co-curricular offering is first class and the overall school environment second to none within the Bweya and Kintende areas.
